First FiveTotals Algo

Same territory as the Hot Streak — the first five innings — but a different question: not who wins them, how many runs get scored. Each offense gets an expected-runs number built from four signals: its season-long F5 scoring rate, its last-14-day hot streak, the opposing starter's recent runs allowed through five, and the park. How much each signal matters isn't hand-picked — the weights were fit on a full season of reconstructed slates. The two team numbers combine into a full run distribution (runs are streaky, so it's wider than a naive model would draw), which prices any over/under the book posts. A bet appears only when the model's probability beats the market's by 5%.
