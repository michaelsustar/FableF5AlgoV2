Algorithmic sports models

DocShuuBets

The math makes the pick. Not a hunch.

Everything published here is the output of a statistical model — no touts, no gut calls, no takes about who wants it more. Each algorithm is built around a specific, testable idea, measured against seasons of real results, then locked down and run every day against live market prices. When a model has an edge, the numbers show it. When it doesn't, the numbers show that too — and both stay posted.

How a model gets here same three gates, every time

01

Built on one idea

Every algorithm starts with a specific claim about what predicts an outcome — recent form, matchup structure, pace, whatever the sport actually rewards — and turns it into numbers a computer can score.

02

Backtested, then frozen

The model is fit on past games, then tested against seasons it never saw during development. If it survives that, its settings are locked. Nothing gets quietly retuned after a bad week — that's how a model fools the person who built it.

03

Graded in the open

Each day's picks are logged at the price shown and graded automatically once the games finish. Every model page carries its own running ledger: record, units, return, and whether its confidence has held up.
